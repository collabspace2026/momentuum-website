Where we typically focus

Performance rarely breaks in just one place.

These are the areas we look at first — not every engagement touches all eight, but broader constraints usually show up across more than one.

Capacity

How much of your team's time is going to work that doesn't need a skilled person doing it.

Customer Journey

What a customer actually experiences from first contact to delivery — and where it breaks down.

Workflow

How work moves between people and systems, and where it stalls or gets duplicated.

Systems

Whether your existing tools are set up to do the job, or quietly working against you.

Reporting

Whether you can see what's really happening, or you're making decisions on gut feel.

Margin Leakage

Where cost, time or price is quietly eroding what a job should be worth.

Role Clarity

Who's actually responsible for what — and where decisions get stuck waiting on one person.

Owner Dependency

How much of the business still runs through you, and what that's costing you.

How engagements are structured

Four phases. Start with the first — go further if it's worth it.

Most engagements start with the Diagnostic alone. Whether you go further depends entirely on what it finds.

1

Optimisation Diagnostic

Current state, a constraint map, quantified priorities and a 90-day program.

2

Improvement Sprint

One cross-functional constraint fixed and measured, typically over 4–8 weeks.

3

Optimisation Program

Several coordinated improvements delivered over 3–6 months.

4

Performance Retainer

Ongoing monthly governance, benefits tracking and a rolling next-improvement cadence.

Every phase has a defined scope and a typical timeframe. Investment is confirmed for your business after a diagnostic conversation — we don't publish pricing here, because the right scope depends on how many workflows and disciplines are involved.

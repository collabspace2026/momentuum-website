The five Proven Solutions

A specific problem. A repeatable fix. A faster path to done.

Each Proven Solution has a defined scope, a standard implementation and a clear outcome — so once we've confirmed it's the right fit for your business, we can move quickly.

Lead Intelligence

Every new lead arrives pre-researched — the details, the context and a suggested next step already pulled together, so your best people spend their time talking to buyers, not googling them.

Best for: businesses where someone manually researches every enquiry before responding.

Customer Reactivation

We turn your existing customer list into a quiet revenue channel — timely, personal outreach at the moments that matter, so past customers come back without anyone chasing them manually.

Best for: businesses sitting on a customer database that isn't being actively worked.

After-Hours Triage

Calls and messages outside business hours get triaged automatically — genuine emergencies reach the right person immediately, and everything else waits until morning.

Best for: businesses where after-hours contact currently means someone's phone going off for things that could wait.

Missed Enquiry Recovery

Every missed call or form gets followed up within minutes, automatically — so a busy afternoon doesn't quietly cost you the job.

Best for: businesses that suspect enquiries are falling through the cracks but don't know how many.

Quote Follow-Up

Quotes get followed up consistently and on schedule, without relying on someone remembering to do it — recovering work that would otherwise go quiet.

Best for: businesses where quotes go out reliably but follow-up depends on whoever has time that week.

Every Proven Solution is available as a fully managed service, from $250/month. Setup is a fixed, indicative range confirmed for your business on a discovery call — we don't publish a generic price list, because the right scope depends on your systems and situation.
